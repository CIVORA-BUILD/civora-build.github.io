Meet the founder

One student.
One big vision.

I'm Mohammed Shahid, a second-year Civil Engineering student and the founder of CIVORA. I started this project with a simple goal: make useful civil engineering tools easier to access.

Building CIVORA while studying means learning new technologies, fixing bugs, testing features and improving the experience step by step. Every challenge is another opportunity to learn.

My vision is to create a helpful platform for civil engineering students, engineers and construction professionals.
