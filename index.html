The person behind CIVORA

One civil engineering student. One big vision.

I'm Mohammed Shahid, a second-year Civil Engineering student and the founder of CIVORA. I started this project to make useful engineering tools more accessible to students and professionals.

Building an app while studying has meant learning new technologies, solving bugs, testing features and improving the experience one step at a time. CIVORA is a work in progress, and every improvement moves the vision forward.
