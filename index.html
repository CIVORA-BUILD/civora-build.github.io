The story behind the project

A civil engineering student building a bigger vision.

I'm Mohammed Shahid TA, a second-year Civil Engineering student and the founder of CIVORA. I started this project to explore how technology could help civil engineering students and professionals access practical tools and learning resources more easily.

Building an app has meant learning Flutter, designing interfaces, connecting services, troubleshooting errors and improving the product one step at a time. CIVORA is both a technology project and part of my journey as an engineer.

  • Learn by building real things
  • Make civil engineering tools more accessible
  • Improve the product through testing and feedback
